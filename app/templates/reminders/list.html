Checking notification permission

Reminders fire only while PVTTSpace is open in a browser tab or as an installed app that is running. When the site is fully closed, the browser cannot wake it, so a reminder shows as due the next time you open it. Background push to a closed browser needs a push server, which this version does not include. You can turn reminder notifications off in Settings.
